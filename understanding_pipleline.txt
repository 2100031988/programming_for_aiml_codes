A typical ML Pipeline includes
-- Data : inspect for common problem
-- Model selection : a candinate model based on data and task
-- Testing and Training : split data into training and test sets
-- Validation : splitiing of training data into reduced training + validation sets and train candinates
-- Apply : evaluate best model on test data

CANDINATE MODEL 
-- A candidate model is simply a machine learning model that you've trained and are considering for use
but haven't officially picked yet. It's a "contender" waiting to prove itself.

Imagine you're hiring a chef:

You invite 5 chefs to cook a dish (these are your candidate models)
You taste each dish and score them (this is EVALUATION)
You pick the best one for your restaurant (this is MODEL SELECTION)

NOISE 
-- unwanted variation that does not reflect true underlying pattern

OUTLIERS
-- arise from mesaurement errors or deviate from others

ERRORS
-- incorrect or invalid errors
-- we can handle noise, errors and outlier through detect % discard strategy ()

FEATURE RELEVANCE
-- Selecting or combining variables that are strongly related to target model perdormance, reduces overfitting
-- Dimensionality reduction methods (PCA, feature selection) help discard weak or redundant features.
